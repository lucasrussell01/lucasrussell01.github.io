I'm a PhD student in elementary particle physics at [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/high-energy-physics/ "ICL"), supported
 by the Schr&ouml;dinger Scholarship.
My research thus far has focused on the CP structure of the Higgs boson's Yukawa coupling to tau leptons, and on machine-learning-based tau reconstruction and identification, at the [Compact Muon Solenoid](https://cms.cern/ "CMS") experiment at CERN.
I am currently writing my PhD thesis, and will be available for new opportunities from February 2027.

A selection of my key publications is available on my [publications page](/publications/#selected-publications) and my CV can be found [here](/cv/).

Here, you can find links to my [GitHub](https://github.com/lucasrussell01 "github") account (with code from several past projects) and my 
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucasrussell01/ "linkedin") and [INSPIRE-HEP](https://inspirehep.net/authors/2686809 'inspire') profiles, 
as well as my [email](mailto:lucas.russell@cern.ch "mail") address if you'd like to get in touch. 

This website is currently being reorganised. In the future, it will include descriptions of some of my work on CMS, primarily focused on studies of the Higgs boson decaying into tau leptons, and machine learning based tau reconstruction/identification. In the meantime, you can find more information in my [CV](/cv/).

My master's thesis on the identification of hadronic tau lepton decays with domain adaptation at CMS is available online
 [CERN-THESIS-2022-126](https://cds.cern.ch/record/2827366/ "Thesis").
